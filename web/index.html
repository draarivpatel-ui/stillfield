      How it works
      
  
      
    
      Each frame is averaged into 2 x 2 CSS px cells in linear light. Every cell tracks its relative luminance and its colour. A change of 0.1 or more, with the darker state under 0.8, is a transition; a change of more than 0.2 in CIE 1976 u'v' to or from a saturated red is a red transition. A flash is two transitions.
      
    
      A cell that makes 7 or more transitions inside one second is flashing more than 3 times a second. The screen fails when those cells cover more than 21,824 CSS px inside any 341 x 256 CSS px field: the normative WCAG 2.2 reading, which is stricter than the 87,296 CSS px in the Understanding note.
      
    
      Video is screened full screen at the 1024 x 768 reference unless you choose a size; GIFs at their own size, looped. Two flashes a second held for 4 s is reported as a warning, as IRIS and ITU-R BT.1702 do, without changing the WCAG result.
